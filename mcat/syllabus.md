# MCAT Content Outline (condensed)

Based on the AAMC content outline. Foundational Concepts (FC) and Content Categories (CC).
Check the official AAMC outline for the full list.

## Chemical and Physical Foundations of Biological Systems (C/P)
- FC4: Physical interactions, motion, forces, work, energy, and the behavior of fluids, gases, and circuits, light and sound.
  - 4A Translational motion, forces, work, energy, equilibrium
  - 4B Importance of fluids for the circulation of blood, gas movement, and gas exchange
  - 4C Electrochemistry and electrical circuits and their elements
  - 4D How light and sound interact with matter
  - 4E Atoms, nuclear decay, electronic structure, and atomic chemical behavior
- FC5: Chemical and physical principles of solutions, molecules, and reactions.
  - 5A Unique nature of water and its solutions
  - 5B Nature of molecules and intermolecular interactions
  - 5C Separation and purification methods
  - 5D Structure, function, and reactivity of biologically relevant molecules
  - 5E Principles of chemical thermodynamics and kinetics

## Critical Analysis and Reasoning Skills (CARS)
- Humanities and social sciences passages, no outside knowledge needed.
- Skills: foundations of comprehension, reasoning within the text, reasoning beyond the text.
- Question types: main idea, detail, inference, function, tone/attitude, application, strengthen/weaken, new information.

## Biological and Biochemical Foundations of Living Systems (B/B)
- FC1: Proteins, nucleic acids, carbohydrates, and lipids assemble into molecules, cells, and organelles.
  - 1A Structure and function of proteins and their constituent amino acids
  - 1B Transmission of genetic information from the gene to the protein
  - 1C Transmission of heritable information from generation to generation, and the processes that increase genetic diversity
  - 1D Principles of bioenergetics and fuel molecule metabolism
- FC2: Highly organized assemblies of molecules, cells, and organs support complex living organisms.
  - 2A Assemblies of molecules, cells, and groups of cells within single cellular and multicellular organisms
  - 2B Structure, growth, physiology, and genetics of prokaryotes and viruses
  - 2C Processes of cell division, differentiation, and specialization
- FC3: Complex systems of tissues and organs sense the internal and external environments.
  - 3A Structure and functions of the nervous and endocrine systems
  - 3B Structure and integrative functions of the main organ systems

## Psychological, Social, and Biological Foundations of Behavior (P/S)
- FC6: Sensing the environment and its effect on behavior.
  - 6A Sensory processing
  - 6B Making sense of the environment (attention, perception, cognition, language)
  - 6C Responding to the world (emotion, stress)
- FC7: Factors that influence behavior and behavior change.
  - 7A Individual influences on behavior (biology, personality, motivation, psychological disorders)
  - 7B Social processes that influence human behavior
  - 7C Attitude and behavior change (learning, theories of attitude)
- FC8: Self-identity and social interaction.
  - 8A Self-identity
  - 8B Social thinking
  - 8C Social interactions
- FC9: Cultural and social differences that influence well-being.
  - 9A Understanding social structure
  - 9B Demographic characteristics and processes
- FC10: Social stratification and access to resources.
  - 10A Social inequality
