## Strategies adopted
Timing
- Science: 60-90 s per question. At 75-90 s, eliminate, guess, flag, move on.
- CARS: 10 min per passage (3.5 read, 6.5 questions), 90 s cap per question. Remaining-time checkpoints from 90:00: 80, 70, 60, 50, 40, 30, 20, 10, 0.
- CARS practice: untimed until accuracy is above 80%, then 12 min cap, then 10 min cap.

CARS reading
- One-sentence-per-paragraph notes failed. Use a 1-2 word mental label per paragraph, track author stance, use an enthusiastic inner voice.
- Write a 3-5 word main idea before questions.
- Highlight only stance words, contrasts, thesis, and stats (under about 20%).
- Question types: Foundations of Comprehension, Reasoning Within the Text, Reasoning Beyond the Text.
- Predict first. Eliminate extreme words, outside knowledge, true-but-contradicts-author, right-words-wrong-meaning. If stuck, pick the choice with fewer assumptions and more text support.
- Weaken: find the author's specific claim, pick what makes it false or backfire. Theme answers paraphrase meaning, not wording.

Sciences
- Figures: TAUT (title, axes, units, trend) before choices. Check each axis before matching shapes.
- Experimental design: state what the hypothesis physically requires, pick the test whose result differs if true vs false. Evidence supports or weakens, never proves.
- Check every clause. "Unique," "only," "all" are red flags. Right only if every clause is true and it answers the stem.

## Content covered
Gen chem
- Oxidation numbers: F always -1; O -2 (peroxide -1, positive with F); H +1 with nonmetals, -1 with metals; groups 1 and 2 fixed; halogens positive only with O or F. Sum equals charge. HOCl: Cl +1.
- Coordination: NH3, H2O, CO neutral; anions add their charge. Cisplatin Pt +2.
- Lewis acid accepts electrons (metal ion); ligand is Lewis base. Lewis is broadest. No proton transfer means Lewis only.
- Ionization removes highest n first (Gd3+ is [Xe]4f7); Aufbau fills lowest first. Paramagnetic: unpaired. Diamagnetic: all paired. Heisenberg: position/momentum uncertainty. Pauli: no identical quantum numbers.
- Bohr: emission drops, absorption jumps. Emission spectrum is bright lines on dark; absorption is dark lines on rainbow.
- Ksp is fixed at a given T; IP uses current concentrations raised to coefficients. IP > Ksp precipitates. Account for dilution. Common ion is a Le Chatelier shift; only T changes K.
- Weak base: BOH <-> B+ + OH-, Kb = [B+][OH-]/[BOH].
- dG = -nFE. Entropy: spread-out energy has more arrangements, so heat flows hot to cold.
- Electrons flow anode to cathode in the wire, opposite conventional current.
- VSEPR: 4 groups + 1 LP seesaw; 4 groups + 2 LP square planar. Watch diatomics (F2) in stoich.

Physics
- Poiseuille: Q scales with r^4 (2x radius = 16x flow); linear in pressure; inverse with viscosity and length.
- Q = dP/R; Q = Av; v = Q/(pi r^2). Arterioles are main resistance; capillaries in parallel.
- Fraction submerged = object density / fluid density. x = ((v0 + v)/2) t.
- F = kqQ/r^2, E = kQ/r^2, V = kQ/r, U = kqQ/r. V = Ed only in uniform fields. Inside a conductor E = 0, V constant.
- Series: same current, voltage divides. Parallel: same voltage, current splits. Removing a series resistor lowers R. Discharging capacitor acts as a voltage source.
- I vs 1/R has slope V. Independent variable on x, measured on y.
- Single slit dark fringes: sin(theta) = m lambda / a.
- Heat loss up with superficial vasodilation and ventilation; vasoconstriction reduces it. Water densest at 4 C.

Organic / biochem
- Functional group priority: carboxylic acid, anhydride, ester, amide, nitrile, aldehyde, ketone, alcohol, amine, ether, alkene/alkyne, alkane.
- Carbs: open-chain numbering carries over; aldose anomeric C1, ketose C2. Anomeric C bonds two O. Pyranose 6-ring, furanose 5-ring. Fructofuranose has C1 and C6 outside the ring. Sucrose alpha 1-2, non-reducing. Haworth: CH2OH up is D; anomeric OH opposite CH2OH is alpha, same side beta. Mutarotation interconverts anomers.
- Glycoproteins: N-linked (Asn, ER), O-linked (Ser/Thr, Golgi). Cerebroside 1 sugar, globoside 2+ neutral sugars, ganglioside has sialic acid. Ceramide uses an amide bond.
- Omega numbering from methyl end; C1 is carboxyl carbon.
- At pH 7.4: Asp, Glu negative; Lys, Arg positive; His pKa ~6; Cys (~8.3) and Tyr (~10.5) deprotonate at high pH. Purely ketogenic: Leu, Lys. Both: Ile, Phe, Trp, Tyr, Thr.
- Km is not Kd except special cases; kcat/Km is efficiency. Competitive: Km up, Vmax same. Noncompetitive: Vmax down, Km same. Uncompetitive: both down.
- Sanger uses ddNTPs (no 3'-OH). Southern DNA, Northern RNA, Western protein. PCNA is the sliding clamp (pol delta lagging, pol epsilon leading, RFC loads it).
- RQ = CO2/O2: carb 1.0, protein ~0.8, fat ~0.7. Chemiosmosis has no NAD+/NADH redox. Prokaryote ETC is in the plasma membrane; glycolysis is cytosolic in all cells. Brain and RBCs use insulin-independent GLUT1.

Physiology / cell bio
- RAAS: renin -> angiotensin I (inactive) -> angiotensin II (vasoconstriction, aldosterone, ADH). Aldosterone reabsorbs Na+ (water follows); ADH reabsorbs water only via aquaporins.
- Autonomic: preganglionic ACh always; postganglionic NE (sympathetic) or ACh (parasympathetic). Positive feedback (oxytocin) stops when stimulus is removed.
- NMJ: ACh. Presynaptic Ca2+ triggers release; SR Ca2+ drives contraction.
- Glucocorticoid effects are broad because receptors are in nearly all cells. Glymphatic clearance is greatest in slow-wave sleep; sleep loss links to Alzheimer's.
- Hydrophilic molecules need help to cross the membrane core. Phospholipid SA: eukaryote > prokaryote > enveloped virus.
- Prokaryotes: naked circular DNA, no introns, no nucleus or membrane organelles, 70S ribosomes, binary fission, single haploid chromosome, 1-5 um.
- Anterior pituitary: FSH, LH, ACTH, TSH tropic; prolactin, endorphins, GH direct.
